Our departments.

Seven departments supporting our BE, ME, MCA and MBA programmes.

01 / ENGINEERING

Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

Computing, software development, databases and intelligent applications.

02 / ENGINEERING

Civil Engineering

Structural design, construction, surveying and infrastructure.

03 / ENGINEERING

Mechanical Engineering

Design, manufacturing, thermal and mechanical systems.

04 / ENGINEERING

Electronic circuits, communication and embedded systems.

Electrical & Electronics (EEE)

Electrical machines, power systems and control engineering.

06 / POSTGRADUATE

Advanced computing, application development and IT systems.

Management, finance, marketing and business analytics.
